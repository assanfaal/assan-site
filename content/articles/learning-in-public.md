I am a junior developer. Not a senior engineer, not a career blogger, just someone building things and learning as I go. For a long time I thought writing was something you do once you already know everything. I have changed my mind about that.

## Learning out loud

Writing forces you to understand. If I cannot explain a decision in a paragraph, I probably do not understand it yet. Putting my thoughts here is a way of checking my own work.

It is also a record. In a year I can come back and see what I thought about a problem before I solved it. That is more useful than a polished retrospective written after the fact.

## What I plan to write about

This is not a coding-only blog. I plan to write about three things:

- **Tech**, the actual work. The problems I hit building web apps, what I tried, what finally worked.
- **Learning**, how I study and build at the same time, what helps and what does not.
- **Life**, the honest parts. Freelancing, deadlines, motivation, the days nothing ships.

## A small promise

I will not pretend to know more than I do. If I am unsure about something I will say so. If I change my mind about an earlier post I will write about that too.

That is it for the first one. If you are reading this, thanks for being here at the start.
